Reflections on Codings

Mideterm Refelction



Across my projects so far, I have tried to create clean layouts while exploring color. I prefer simple, clear sans-serif typefaces like Futura, and compositions that use strong contrasts in scale and color without placing too much text on one screen. Another shared feature is encouraging users to discover content gradually through exploration rather than showing everything at once. While Project 1 focused on sound, Projects 2 and 3 emphasized visual enjoyment. In Project 2, I paid particular attention to how colors connected and worked together. This is my first experience with coding, so even understanding the basic structures of HTML and CSS has been challenging. I still rely on AI to help with things I do not understand, but my goal is to build a website independently from start to finish by the end of the semester. Next, I would like to experiment with incorporating animations made in After Effects into a website. I had hoped to explore this in Project 3, but I already had a clear idea of what I wanted to create and did not find room for it. It is something I look forward to trying in a future project.
